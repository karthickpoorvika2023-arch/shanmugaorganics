Exactly what is stored here

Local storage keys used by this site:

  • so_cart_v1 — items, sizes, quantities
  • so_wish_v1 — saved product slugs
  • so_orders_v1 — demo orders you placed
  • so_addr_v1 — the address you typed at checkout
  • so_recent_v1 — last products viewed
  • so_news_v1 — newsletter opt-in flag
  • so_coupon_v1 — applied coupon code
  • so_lang_v1 — English or Tamil preference
Manage or erase this data
Because this is a demo build with no server, the "orders" in your account never reached us and nothing will be shipped. Do not enter a real card number anywhere on this site — there is nothing behind the payment step.

What we collect, and why

In the real shop we collect only what an order needs: your name, delivery address, phone number and email. Phone and address go on the courier label. Email carries the confirmation and tracking. That is the whole purpose, and we do not use it for anything else without asking you.

What we do not collect

  • No card numbers, CVVs or UPI PINs — the gateway handles those and we never see them.
  • No date of birth, gender, income bracket or family details. An order does not need them.
  • No advertising trackers, no Facebook pixel, no cross-site profiling.
  • No location beyond the PIN code you type for delivery.
  • No data from anyone we know to be under 18.

Cookies and fonts

This storefront sets no tracking cookies. It does use browser local storage, which is similar technology but stays on your machine and is never transmitted. The one external request the site makes is to Google Fonts for the Inter, Playfair Display and Noto Sans Tamil typefaces; that request tells Google your IP address and browser, as any font request would. If you would rather avoid it, the site remains fully usable with the request blocked — it falls back to your system fonts.

Who else sees your data

Three kinds of people, each for one narrow purpose:

  • Couriers — Professional Couriers, DTDC, Delhivery and India Post get your name, address and phone so they can deliver the parcel.
  • The payment gateway — gets what it needs to take the payment.
  • Our accountant and the tax authority — get invoices, because GST law requires it.

We do not sell, rent or trade customer lists. We have never done it and we are not going to start — in a business built on people trusting a name, it would be the stupidest possible trade.

How long we keep things

Invoices and order records: eight years, because tax law requires it. Delivery addresses: until you ask us to remove them, or three years after your last order. Newsletter email: until you unsubscribe, which is one click in any email we send. WhatsApp conversations about claims: one year, so we can look back at what was agreed.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a correction, ask for deletion, or withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Write to hello@shanmugaorganics.in and we will act within 30 days, usually within two. We may keep invoice records we are legally required to keep even after a deletion request, and we will tell you if that applies.

Security, stated honestly

The site is served over HTTPS and payments go through a PCI-compliant gateway. Order paperwork at the shop sits in a locked cabinet, and only two people have the key. No system is perfect; if we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell affected customers directly rather than burying a notice on this page.
