A few notes worth reading before you cook

Wood-pressed oil behaves differently

It has a lower smoke point than refined oil and a much stronger aroma. Heat it on medium, not high, and give it a moment before the mustard seeds go in. Groundnut oil suits deep frying and poriyal; nallennai belongs in podi, pickle and rice mixes.

Millets need soaking

Kambu, varagu and thinai want 4–6 hours in water, or overnight for kambu koozh. Skip it and the grain stays chalky at the centre no matter how long you boil it.

Unpolished rice takes more water

Mappillai samba and other single-polished rices need roughly 1 : 3 rice to water and about 40 minutes, against 1 : 2 for supermarket rice. Soak for an hour and the cook time drops by a third.

Sathu maavu is a food, not a supplement

Two heaped spoons in 200 ml of water or milk, boiled for four minutes while stirring — raw powder in warm milk will always taste of raw flour. Add jaggery at the end, off the heat.
