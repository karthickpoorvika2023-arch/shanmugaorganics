How it works

செயல்முறை

STEP 1You tell us the quantity

Form, phone or WhatsApp — whichever is easier. We usually reply the same working day, always within 24 hours.

STEP 2Samples, if you want them

500 ml of any oil or 1 kg of any rice, free, once per buyer. Freight at cost. Most kitchens ask for this and they should.

STEP 3Rate sheet and terms

A dated quote valid for 14 days, with slab pricing. First order is advance; after three clean cycles we move you to 15-day credit.

STEP 4Standing order

Fixed weekly or fortnightly dispatch, one consolidated invoice a month, and a named person on our side you can call.
