Returns & Refunds

Short version: if we got it wrong, we fix it at our cost. Last updated 1 August 2025.

48-hour damage window

48 மணி நேரம்

Leaked, broken or short-shipped? Send a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery and we replace or refund. No return shipping.

7-day return on sealed goods

7 நாட்கள்

Unopened, seal intact, original pack — return within 7 days for a refund minus the actual return freight.

Opened food cannot come back

திறந்த உணவு பொருட்கள்

Food-safety rules, not a loophole. But if an opened product is genuinely bad, tell us — see "quality complaints" below.

We take these back

  • Wrong item shipped. Our packing error — we collect it and send the right one, both legs at our cost.
  • Damaged or leaking in transit. Photo within 48 hours; replacement or full refund, your choice.
  • Short shipment. Something on the invoice that is not in the box. We dispatch the missing item immediately.
  • Sealed and unopened, within 7 days. Changed your mind — refund minus the actual return freight.
  • Under the printed net weight. Weigh it, send a photo of the scale; we refund the shortfall or replace the pack.
  • Past its best-before on arrival. Should never happen. Full refund and we investigate the batch.

We cannot take these back

  • Opened food, oil, ghee or honey. Once the seal is broken we cannot resell or verify it. FSSAI rules for a manufacturing unit are firm on this.
  • Used soap, hair oil or powders. Same reason. Unopened bars are fine.
  • Custom gift hampers and private-label runs. Made to your artwork, so there is no second buyer.
  • Bulk tins and 25 kg bags that have been decanted. Trade orders are inspected on delivery; sign only after you have checked.
  • "It tastes different from refined oil." Wood-pressed oil has a strong aroma and settles a little sediment. That is what it is supposed to do — read the product page before ordering if you are new to it.
  • Anything reported after 7 days for change-of-mind, or after 48 hours for transit damage.

How to raise a claim

STEP 1Photograph it before unpacking further

The outer carton, the courier label, and the damaged item. Three photos settle almost every claim.

STEP 2WhatsApp with your order number

+91 638 004 0001. Or use the contact form if you prefer email.

STEP 3We decide within one working day

No committee, no escalation queue. You get a yes-or-no and a reason.

STEP 4Replacement or refund

Replacements dispatch the same day. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Keep the packing material until the claim closes. Couriers occasionally ask to inspect, and a claim without the carton is much harder for us to win on your behalf — though we will still honour ours.

Refund timelines

Counted from the moment we approve the claim, not from when you sent it:

  • UPI — 1 to 3 working days.
  • Card — 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank's settlement cycle.
  • Net banking — 3 to 5 working days.
  • Cash on delivery — bank transfer within 3 working days, once you send account details. The ₹40 COD fee is refunded only if the fault was ours.
  • Store credit — instant, and we add 5% if you choose it over a refund.

Quality complaints on opened products

We cannot accept the physical return, but we do want to know. Send the batch number from the label and describe what is wrong. If a batch has a genuine fault — rancid oil, a fermented pickle, weevils in rice — we refund it, and we pull the rest of that batch from the shelf while we check. This has happened three times in sixteen years and each time it was a real problem worth finding.

Cancellations

Cancel free of charge any time before dispatch — reply to the confirmation email or call the shop. Once the courier has collected, it becomes a return under the rules above. Store-pickup orders can be cancelled up to the moment you collect them.

Wholesale and bulk

Trade orders are inspected at delivery and signed for. After signature we accept claims only for concealed damage reported within 24 hours, with photos. Rate disputes are settled against the dated quote you were sent — which is why every quote carries a date.

Consumer rights

Nothing here limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. If you think we have handled a claim badly, ask for it to be escalated to the proprietor; that request is always granted.
