Finding us

On Dharapuram Road, about 1.8 km from Erode Junction and 400 m past the Perundurai Road signal. Look for the green board with the leaf — we are between a hardware shop and a bakery, first floor above the presses.

Parking

Two-wheeler parking in front. Cars park in the side lane beside the bakery, which is free but tight after 6 PM on weekends.

Unit visits

Saturdays 6 AM – 10 AM, walk in, no appointment. The chekku presses are running then and you can watch a full batch from seed to bottle.
