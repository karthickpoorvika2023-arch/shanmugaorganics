The details

Dispatch days

We ship Monday to Saturday. Sunday and local festival days are packing days only — the courier does not collect, so a Sunday order dispatches Monday. During Pongal and Diwali week, add one day to everything below; volumes triple and we would rather tell you than surprise you.

Couriers we use

Professional Couriers and DTDC for Tamil Nadu and the south, Delhivery for the rest of India, and India Post for PIN codes the private couriers do not reach. You get the tracking number by SMS and email when the label is generated, and you can follow it on our Track Order page.

Address accuracy

Please include a landmark and a reachable phone number. Couriers in smaller towns call before delivering, and an unreachable number is the single most common reason a parcel comes back to us. Re-dispatch after a failed delivery costs the shipping charge again.

Weight-heavy orders

Orders over 15 kg — say four 5 L oil cans, or two 10 kg rice bags — may ship as two consignments arriving a day apart. We do not charge twice for that; it is a courier weight-slab decision, not an extra order.

Summer and ghee

Between March and June, ghee will liquefy in transit. That is physics, not spoilage — it re-solidifies in a cool place and the quality is unaffected. If a jar actually leaks, it is covered by our returns policy.

What we cannot ship

We do not ship outside India. We also cannot ship glass-bottled oils to island territories, because the transit handling breaks too many of them — HDPE packs go there instead, and we will swap the pack and tell you if you order glass.
