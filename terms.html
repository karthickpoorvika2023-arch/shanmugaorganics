All prices are in Indian Rupees and include GST unless the page says otherwise. Agricultural prices move — oil, in particular, tracks the groundnut and copra market — so prices can change without notice. The price that applies to you is the one shown when you place the order.

If a price is obviously wrong through a typing or system error, we are not obliged to sell at it. We will contact you, offer the correct price, and refund you in full if you would rather not proceed. MRP shown struck through is the maximum retail price for that pack, not a competitor's price.

5. Product descriptions and claims

We write our own product pages and try hard to be accurate. Two honest caveats:

  • Natural variation is real. Colour, aroma and sediment differ between batches of wood-pressed oil, and grain size differs between harvests. Photographs and illustrations on this site are indicative.
  • "Organic" is not a blanket claim. We are not a certified-organic operator across every line. Where a product comes from conventional farming, its page says so. Where a farmer follows organic practice without certification, we say that too, rather than implying paperwork we do not hold.

Net weights are as printed on the pack, measured at packing. Oils are sold by volume at packing temperature.
